# Nabo, five-minute demo script

Open `index.html` in a browser, full screen, zoomed so all five phones fit. Press **Start over** before you begin. One person drives, one person talks.

## 1. The problem (45 seconds)

Say it in your own words. Roughly:

> Someone who knows a few people in Oslo struggles to actually meet them, because every hangout depends on one person asking and coordinating. After a few "can't that day" replies, they stop asking and assume nobody wants to.
>
> We heard it around this table today. New in the city, one-sided effort. Friends with kids and cabins. "Coffee sometime" that never happens. The thing that kills it is initiating and coordinating.
>
> So we moved the asking away from the person, onto an agent. Nobody has to ask. Nobody is turned down in public.
>
> And it is not Meetup. Nobody creates events on Nabo. The city is already full of things happening: gigs, climbing nights, volunteer shifts. Nabo scans them, picks people who fit, and sends them together.

## 2. Who it is for (30 seconds)

Point at the left phone.

> This is Jonas. Made up. Moved here in February, into jazz and cycling, knows a few people, has stopped asking. The other phones are Sara, Erik and Amina, already on Nabo. The audience sees all five screens. Each person only sees their own.

## 3. Show it working (3 minutes)

**Onboarding.** Drive: tap two interests, tap **Next**, tap a free evening, **Next**, **Use a photo**, **Next**, **Fair, I'm open to suggestions**. (Short on time: press **Skip onboarding** at the top.)

> Four questions. Interests, free evenings, a photo, and the deal: Nabo proposes, you only say yes or no, and if you say yes and don't show, Nabo pauses you. Effort on purpose. It keeps flakers out.

**The card.** The jazz card appears on four phones. Point at the why-you box.

> This gig is on anyway. Nabo found it among a couple of hundred things in Oslo this week and picked four people who fit. Same card, same time, to all four. Nobody organised it. No "maybe". If you say no, nobody knows you were asked.

**Jonas says yes.** Tap **Yes** on Jonas's phone.

> Jonas is in, if it happens. Sara, Erik and Amina don't know he said yes.

**The others answer.** Press **Let the others answer**. Watch Sara yes, Erik no, Amina yes. Three phones flip to "Friday is on".

> Erik said no. Nothing happened on anyone else's screen. That is the point. Three said yes, so it's on for three, at the same moment, and now they see who's coming with a face and a few lines each. Not a pit of strangers. Erik is simply not mentioned.

**The paused phone.** Point at Tomas, right.

> Tomas said yes last week and didn't go. He's paused until he attends one thing. Nobody else is told. A yes is a promise.

**The morning after.** Press **The morning after**. Tap **See again** on Sara, press **Done**.

> Saturday. Who would you like to see again? Jonas picks Sara. She doesn't know. Nobody ever sees a pick unless it's mutual.

Press **Let the others pick**. Pair cards appear.

> Sara picked Jonas too, so Nabo proposes coffee Tuesday to both, as its own idea. Sara also picked Amina, and that was mutual too. Jonas is never told about that one.

**Close the loop.** Tap **Yes** on Jonas's coffee card, press **Let the others answer**.

> Both said yes. It's on. Look at "your week" at the bottom: jazz Friday, coffee Tuesday. Jonas asked nobody, chased nobody, and nobody was turned down in public.

Scroll to the ledger at the bottom if someone asks about privacy. Every message Nabo sent, and to whom, is listed.

## 4. What next (30 seconds)

> Three things we'd do next. First, groups to one-to-one is the loop; the agent should learn from who clicks and who flakes, a quiet social score nobody sees. Second, organisations: a shelter that needs four people Thursday is the same engine. Third, scale: what this looks like for a whole neighbourhood, and what breaks, cliques, exclusion, trust in the agent.

## If something goes wrong

- **Start over** resets everything.
- **Skip onboarding** jumps straight to the card.
- If a phone looks stuck, the top bar in orange says what state Nabo is in. Read it out.
- The page is one HTML file with no network. If the browser is open, it works.
