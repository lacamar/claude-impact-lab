# Claude Impact Lab

Read BRIEF.md first. It is the day's brief for you: schedule, habits, how to interview the team, what a good result is.

## Deliverable

- One self-contained HTML file in this repo root, opens in any browser, nothing to install, no build step, no external dependencies.
- Made-up example data only. Never real names or personal details.
- One screen that works beats many that don't.

## Working with the team

- Ask one multiple-choice question at a time with AskUserQuestion, three or four options, always a free-text way out. Several people share one screen; give them a moment to agree.
- Never guess who is in the team or what they know. Ask.
- Open the page in a browser and try it as a first-time user before saying it is done.
- One change at a time, say what changed, in plain language.
- Ask before installing anything or using accounts or keys.
- After each working version, end with two or three ways to take it further, small and big, and what each would show.
